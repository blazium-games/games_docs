# Refunds

## The rule

A purchase can be refunded until **7 days** have passed or you have played for **2 hours**, whichever comes first. Your [library](https://blazium.games/library) shows each game's refund window and playtime.

**Donations are not refundable.** A donation is a gift to the developer, not a purchase. The only exceptions are where the law requires a refund, or where we reverse a donation tied to fraud.

## How to ask

Email [support@blazium.games](mailto:support@blazium.games) from your account email with the game name and, if you have it, the purchase ID from your wallet transactions. Refunds are not available through the site buttons or MCP.

We may refund outside the window at our discretion, for example for a broken build.

## What you get back

A refund is always credited to your account balance, never to a card: the price plus tax, minus the platform fee. The card processing fee and the platform fee are not returned. Nothing is sent to a card, a bank, or a USDC address. To get money out, request a payout.

- **Paid by card or USDC:** credited to your account balance as available earnings.
- **Paid from balance:** credited to your account balance as credit, which cannot be cashed out.

The license is revoked when the refund goes through, and the game can no longer be downloaded.

A paid copyright-notice fee is not a purchase refund. Filing starts the review, so the work is performed when the fee is paid. Support does not refund that fee, whether the claim is accepted, rejected, resolved, closed, or withdrawn.

## What happens for the developer

The sale's earnings are taken back from the developer's balance. The platform fee is not returned.

## Chargebacks

Disputing a card payment with your bank revokes the license while the dispute is open. Please contact support first; it is usually faster.
