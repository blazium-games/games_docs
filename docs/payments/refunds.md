# Refunds

## The rule

A purchase can be refunded until **7 days** have passed or you have played for **2 hours**, whichever comes first. Your [library](https://blazium.games/library) shows each game's refund window and playtime.

**Donations are not refundable.** A donation is a gift to the developer, not a purchase. The only exceptions are where the law requires a refund, or where we reverse a donation tied to fraud.

## How to ask

Open your [library](https://blazium.games/library), pick the game, and choose **Request a refund**. It appears while the game is inside its refund window. The request goes to Blazium staff, who can accept or decline it. You keep the game and can keep playing while it is open. If nobody decides, the refund is **approved automatically after 64 business hours**. Business hours are weekdays from 8:00am to 5:00pm Pacific.

A request is refused when:

- the 7-day or 2-hour window has already passed (`4101`);
- the purchase is not paid, or a request on it was already decided (`4102`);
- you have a cash-out open (`4103`);
- the game was replaced by an upgrade or another edition (`4104`).

A refund is a human action on the website. An AI agent cannot ask for a refund or check on one through MCP: the refund routes answer `4084`, and the servers do not list `request_refund` or `get_refund_request` for your account. Blazium staff can turn MCP refunds on for one account. Then the two tools appear for that account, and each request still waits for the account owner's approval. You can also email [support@blazium.games](mailto:support@blazium.games) from your account email with the game name and, if you have it, the purchase ID from your wallet transactions.

We may refund outside the window at our discretion, for example for a broken build.
## What you get back

A refund is always credited to your account balance, never to a card: the price plus tax, minus the platform fee. The card processing fee and the platform fee are not returned. Nothing is sent to a card, a bank, or a USDC address. The credit can be spent on the platform but cannot be cashed out.

- **Paid by card or USDC:** credited to your account balance as credit.
- **Paid from balance:** credited to your account balance as credit, which cannot be cashed out.

The license is revoked when the refund goes through, and the game can no longer be downloaded.

A paid copyright-notice fee is not a purchase refund. Filing starts the review, so the work is performed when the fee is paid. Support does not refund that fee, whether the claim is accepted, rejected, resolved, closed, or withdrawn.

## What happens for the developer

The sale's earnings are taken back from the developer's balance. The platform fee is not returned.

## Chargebacks

Disputing a card payment with your bank revokes the license while the dispute is open. Please contact support first; it is usually faster.
