# Buying games

## Buy a game

On a paid game's store page, choose **Buy**:

- **Pay by card:** you are sent to Stripe Checkout. The price, tax for your location, and a card processing fee are shown before you pay. After paying you come back to a page that confirms the purchase.
- **Pay from balance:** the page shows a quote with the price, tax, and total. Confirm it and the total is taken from your balance at once, with no card fee. If the total changes before you confirm (for example, the price changed), you are asked to confirm again.

Either way you get a license for the game, listed in your [Library](https://blazium.games/library).

## Donate

Free games that accept donations show a **Donate** box. Pick an amount from $1.00 to $500.00 and pay by card or from your balance. Donations do not change your access to the game and are not refundable.

## Download

Downloads need a verified email. Paid games also need a license. Download buttons on store pages and in your library give you a short-lived link (5 minutes) to the file.

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Sent to log in | Log in, then press Download again |
| Sent to verify your email | Enter the code sent to your account email |
| Sent back to the store page to buy | Buy the game, then download |
| "Still being scanned" | The file was just uploaded. Try again in a few minutes |

## Playtime

Your library shows how long you have played each game, as reported by the Blazium Games app while a game runs. Playtime matters for refunds: a purchase stops being refundable after 2 hours of play. See [Refunds](./refunds.md).

## Purchase history

Every purchase, from a card or the balance, is listed in your [wallet](https://blazium.games/settings/wallet) transactions.
