# About

Blazium Games is a place to publish and download games, applications, mods, and assets. Players keep a library, and developers host a page for each project. Builds are uploaded with the chauffeur CLI and served from this platform.

Divine Games, Inc. operates Blazium Games.

Publishing an account is free. When a game is sold or a donation is made, the fee is fixed: $0.25 plus 8% of the price, taken from the developer. There is no seller-chosen revenue share. The rest of the payment rules are on [Payments](./payments/index.md).

Downloads for a published project are hosted here. A player who owns a game, or who downloads a free one, gets the files from Blazium Games.

Help with an account or a payment is on the [support page](https://blazium.games/support). The [introduction](./intro.md) lists the other guides.
