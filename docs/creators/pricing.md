# Pricing

An account is free. A project can be free, or it can have a fixed price. A free project can also accept an optional donation. The fee on a sale or a donation is $0.25 plus 8%, taken from the developer. Card fees and tax are shown to the buyer before they pay. Paying from a balance adds nothing extra. See [Payments](../payments/index.md) and [Selling](../payments/selling.md).

There is no open revenue share, no pay-any-amount-above-a-minimum, and no separate price per file.

## Editions and bundles

A listing can have editions: standard, deluxe, beta access, and a bundle of 1 to 10 of your own listings. Someone who already owns a cheaper edition can pay the difference. Details are on [Selling](../payments/selling.md).

## Patron tiers

A page can have up to 12 patron tiers. A higher tier includes the lower tier's games, locked posts, and patron-only sales. Monthly tiers renew by card. A one-time tier is paid by card or from the balance. The fee is the same $0.25 plus 8%. A membership payment is not refundable. Access starts after the 12 hour payment check. The full rules are on [Patron memberships](../payments/patrons.md).

Cash-out is USD through Stripe Connect, or USDC on Base or Solana from the wallet page. See [USDC](../payments/wallet-and-cash-out.md#usdc).
