# Creator FAQ

## What does it cost?

An account is free. A sale or a donation pays a fixed fee of $0.25 plus 8%, taken from the developer. Prices, donations, editions, and patron tiers are on [Pricing](./pricing.md). The fee table is on [Payments](../payments/index.md).

## Who handles tax?

Stripe Tax is added for the buyer where Blazium Games is registered. Payout details, including the tax information Stripe asks for, are on [Accepting payments](../payments/index.md) and [Wallet and cash-out](../payments/wallet-and-cash-out.md).

## Who owns the project?

You keep ownership of what you upload. The [terms](https://blazium.games/terms-of-service) are the agreement for using the platform.

## Are my files changed before download?

Files are served as uploaded. Upload them with [chauffeur](../cli/index.md). The site does not accept build uploads in the browser.

## Can I give someone a copy without a purchase?

Yes. [Download keys](../storefront/team-and-keys.md) and [redeem a key](../storefront/redeem-a-key.md) cover that.

## Can I embed the game?

A browser build can be embedded. See [Embed](../storefront/embed.md) and [Play in the browser](../web-player.md).

## Can the page be adult?

Adult pages stay hidden until a player opts in. The rules are in [Content rules](../content-rules.md).

## What do I see about a buyer?

You see sales and download counts, not a buyer's card. [Download analytics](../download-analytics.md) and the dashboard analytics view are the reports. Payment records stay with the [wallet](../payments/wallet-and-cash-out.md).
