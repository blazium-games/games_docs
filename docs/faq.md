# FAQ

## How do I find a game?

Open [Browse](https://blazium.games/browse) and search, or start from the shelves on the home page. The [find a game](./storefront/find-a-game.md) guide covers filters and shelves.

## Where are the games I own?

They are in your [library](https://blazium.games/library). See [Your library](./storefront/library.md).

## I paid and the game is not in my library

Sign in with the same account you paid with. A card purchase can take a moment to show up. If it is still missing, use the [support page](https://blazium.games/support) and include the email on the account. Buying itself is described in [How buying works](./payments/buying.md).

## How do I report a page?

Use the report link on the page, or the [report guide](./storefront/report.md). Copyright claims use the [DMCA](./storefront/dmca.md) form, which has its own fee.

## How do I delete my account?

Account deletion is in [Settings](https://blazium.games/settings). What is kept and for how long is in the [privacy policy](https://blazium.games/privacy-policy).
