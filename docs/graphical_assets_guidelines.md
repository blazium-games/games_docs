# Graphical Assets Guidelines

When listing your product on blazium.games, you provide a thumbnail, a cover and gallery screenshots.
These are displayed in different places on blazium.games.

:::info[Image rules]

- **Formats:** PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP. The format is read from the file itself, not its name.
- **Size:** each side must be between 512 and 2048 px.
- **File size:** up to 10 MB.
- **Aspect ratio:** the store shows every image at 16:9, so use 16:9 to avoid cropping.

Upload images on the game's edit page on the website, or with [`chauffeur media`](./cli/media.md) and the game's deploy key.

:::

## Thumbnail Image
- **Size:** 1280x720 recommended (16:9).
- **Usage:** Used when listing the product on the home page, browse page and similar pages.
- **Design:** For best results use the product's key art and logo.

![Example thumbnail: key art with the game logo](/img/graphical_assets_examples/thumbnail.png)

## Cover Image
- **Size:** 1024x576 recommended (16:9).
- **Usage:** Used in the product page side bar.
- **Design:** The product's logo should be easily legible **even at smaller sizes**.

![Example cover: the game logo on a dark background](/img/graphical_assets_examples/cover.png)

## Screenshots Images
- **Size:** 1920x1080 recommended (16:9). Larger images are rejected, so scale 4K captures down to 2048 px wide or less.
- **Usage:** Screenshots are displayed on your store page. You must provide at least 4 screenshots of your product.
- **Design:** Screenshots should exclusively show the usage of the product (gameplay, tool or asset being used).

![Example screenshot: gameplay](/img/graphical_assets_examples/screenshot.png)
