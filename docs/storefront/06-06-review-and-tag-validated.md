What can go wrong

- Your review means this account already has a review. These pictures do not show a heading Review this game.
- short-runs × and hard × remove your tag. They are not the public tag. hard is not in the public list.
- Suggest, Update review, Delete, Edit reply, and Reply were not used. The pictures do not show the page after those buttons.
- Nova Studio does not get the review form or the tag box. The line is the playtime line, and the reply links are Edit reply and Reply.
- Signed out, you can read the reviews. You do not get the form. The page does not redirect. Log in in the tags line is mark 16. Log in on the beta line is not.
- The account chip is not in these pictures. Do not look for Pixel or Nova Studio in the corner.
- Words in the details list, such as daily-runs or cozy, are not the Players say tags.
