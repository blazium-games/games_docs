# Your library

![The library with two games](/img/storefront/library.png)

Open your library from the account menu: **Library**, under the player items. It lists every game you bought or were given, with:

- The developer.
- Your play time, or "Not played yet".
- **Bought** or **Granted** and the date (UTC).
- "Refundable until ... UTC. Contact support" while a refund is still possible. See [Refunds](../payments/refunds.md).

## Filter the list

The chips above the cards narrow the list:

| Chip | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Everything you own. |
| **Ready to play** | Games with a web build or a download. |
| **Browser** | Games with a web build. |
| **Download** | Games with a download. |
| **In development** | Games still in development or at the prototype stage. |

If nothing matches, click **Show all**.

## Get a game

Each card has buttons for what the game offers:

- **Download** opens the game's store page at its downloads. See [Buy and download a game](./buy-and-download.md#download).
- **Play** appears for games with a web build and also opens the store page at its downloads.
- **Store page** opens the store page.

You can also install and update your games with the [desktop app](./desktop-app.md).

## An empty library

A new account shows "Your library is empty." with a **Browse games** button. Free games are added the first time you download them, and keys are added when you [redeem them](./redeem-a-key.md).
