# Find a game and download or buy it

The pictures use sample data. Garden Tower, Starfall Drift, Moss Garden, Hollow Deep, Color Palette Mod, Ember Racer, Nova Studio, Pixel, and Mothlight are samples. The prices are sample amounts too.

1. On Home, select Play Now under the game you want. It opens that game's store page. It does not start the game. In this sample, Play Now is under Garden Tower. Under the button the page says PLAY IN BROWSER and Free web. That means you can play it in the browser, and it has no price.
2. Select Browse in the header when you want to search instead of the featured game.
Picture 1 (/img/storefront/home-anon.png): 1 is Play Now, 2 is Browse.

3. On Browse, select the search box and type. The box says Search for games, mods and tools when it is empty. This picture is not empty. The word in the box is drift. 3 is in the box, to the right of that word.
4. The chips under the box are All, New, Updated, Development, Multiplayer, and Browser. 4 is All, and All is the filled chip, so All is the filter that is on. This list is from the search word, not from Browser. Select Browser only when you want browser games. Type is already on the page, under the chips: Game, Application, Mod, Plugin, Tool, Game asset, and Development asset. More filters does not open Type. Clear all removes the filters. The line under the filters says 1 to 3 of 3 in this sample. Best Match, at the right of the search box, is the sort menu.
5. Select the game's picture. That opens its store page. 5 is the Starfall Drift picture, the middle card. The same search also shows Color Palette Mod and Ember Racer. Color Palette Mod's line on the page mentions Starfall Drift. Ember Racer's visible line does not say drift, so this picture does not show why that card matched.
Picture 2 (/img/storefront/browse-search.png): 3 is the search box, to the right of drift. 4 is All. 5 is the Starfall Drift picture.

6. On a paid game, while you are signed out, the buy button says Log in to buy. Starfall Drift shows DOWNLOAD and EXTERNAL next to the creator. DOWNLOAD means there is a file to download. EXTERNAL means the game also points somewhere else. The info box says Platforms windows, linux, Price $9.99, and Session 1h. The price column says From $9.99. Standard is $9.99, and the line under it says The full game. Deluxe is $14.99, and the line under it says The game, the soundtrack and the art book. Those edition choices are not marked. Under the button the page says Downloads unlock after you buy the game. This game does not show Play in browser. Selecting Log in to buy opens the login screen. Login is its own guide. Stop here if you are signed out.
Picture 3 (/img/storefront/game-anon.png): 6 is Log in to buy.

Pixel already owns Starfall Drift, so Starfall Drift cannot show this button. 7 is Buy for $12.99 by card. The price on that page is $12.99. The corner reads Pixel. The page shows DOWNLOAD next to mothlight, and the info box says Platforms windows, linux, Price $12.99, and Session 3h.
8. Or select Pay from balance when that button shows money available. 8 is Pay from balance ($15.00 available). On this page that pays for Hollow Deep. It is not an upgrade. The note under the buttons says card payments add a processing fee shown at checkout, tax is added where required, and refunds are available within 7 days if you have played less than 2 hours. Under that, the page says Downloads unlock after you buy the game, and it lists windows amd64 with Version 1.0.3 and 2026-09-25. Those rows are not download buttons you can use yet. Player reviews on this page are a different guide.
Picture 4 (/img/storefront/game-buy-player.png): 7 is Buy for $12.99 by card, 8 is Pay from balance ($15.00 available).

9. If you already own an edition, the price column says so. On Starfall Drift this sample says You own the Standard edition. The column still says From $9.99. Select Upgrade for $14.99 by card only if you want Deluxe. That $14.99 is the sample difference, not a new full price. The line under Deluxe says you pay the difference. Skip this button if you only want the copy you already own.
10. Or select Pay from balance ($15.00 available) on that same Starfall Drift page. That is also an upgrade payment, not a download. The same note about the processing fee, tax, and refunds is under these buttons.

18. Select Play when the card has it. 18 is Play on Starfall Drift. Play opens the store page. Store page on a library card opens the game's page again. The chips All, Ready to play, Browser, Download, and In development are on this page. All is filled. They are not marked.

What can go wrong

Signed out on a paid game, the buy control is Log in to buy, and the downloads stay locked until you own it.
A search can match more than the game you meant. This sample search shows three listings. The picture does not show why Ember Racer matched.
All is the chip that is on in the search picture. Browser is not.
An upgrade button is for a higher edition. Skip it if you only want the copy you already own.
A download whose name includes beta is a test build. The regular build has no beta in the name.
Older versions, under a regular build, is a closed menu that reads Choose An Older Build. This picture does not list the older builds.
The Starfall Drift receipt is not what you get by buying Hollow Deep.
