What can go wrong

- The settings pictures are scrolled. The account chip is not in those frames. Pixel and Nova Studio are only on the menu pictures.
- Pixel's DEVELOPER section is Publish your games and MCP. Nova Studio's is Dashboard, Analytics, and MCP. Do not look for Publish your games on the Nova Studio menu.
- Turn on developer mode was not used. The On badge and the date 2026-06-25 were already on the sample account.
- Turn off developer mode was not used.
- Neither checkbox was used.
- Publish your games, Dashboard, and Analytics were not opened.
- Adult content, the left settings list, and the other menu rows are other guides.
- Signed out, there is no settings page. The picture is the login page, and that form was not used.
