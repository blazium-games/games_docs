# Sign up and sign in

You need to be at least 13 to use Blazium Games. Buying, adding money and cashing out need you to be 18, or to have a parent or guardian who accepts the [Terms of Service](https://blazium.games/terms-of-service). Uploading or publishing needs you to be 18 or otherwise able to enter a contract.

## Create an account with email

1. Click **Sign up** in the header.
2. Fill in **Email**, **Username** and **Password**.
   - Usernames are 4 to 32 letters, numbers or hyphens, and start and end with a letter or number. Your username becomes your developer page address, `username.blazium.games`, so some names are reserved.
   - Passwords are 10 to 128 characters.
3. Tick "I accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy".
4. Click **Sign up**.
5. Enter the 6-digit code we email you and click **Confirm**. The code works for 10 minutes.

If the code doesn't arrive within a minute, check your spam or junk folder for mail from noreply@blazium.games. **Send another code** sends a new one after a short wait.

## Sign in with email

1. Click **Log in** and enter your **Email** and **Password**.
2. Click **Log in**.
3. If we ask for a code, enter the one from your email or your authenticator app.

If you came from another page, you return to it after signing in.

"Your session expired. Log in again to continue." means you were signed out after a while. Sign in again and you continue where you were.

## Sign in with GitHub, X or Discord

Click **GitHub**, **X** or **Discord** on the sign-up or login page.

- **First time with this service:** if it isn't linked to an account yet, we ask you to **Finish your account** with an email and password, then **Send code** to confirm the email. If the email is already registered, you confirm that account's password instead, and the service is linked to it.
- **Already linked:** you may be asked to **Confirm your password**, and then for a code.

Some accounts are asked to choose a second step. **Set up an authenticator app** uses a phone app for codes; **Email me a code instead** keeps using emailed codes. You can change this later.

To link or unlink services, see [Linked accounts](../linked-accounts.md).

## Finish setting up

After signing in, you may see a **Finish signing in** page. It walks you through whatever is still missing:

- **Finish setting up your account**: choose a username, and a password if you don't have one.
- **Protect your account with an authenticator app**: set one up, or click **Skip for now** to keep using emailed codes.

Save the recovery codes you see after setting up an authenticator. They are shown only once. More in [Account security](../account-security.md).

**Log out instead** is always available on this page.

## Updated terms

When the Terms of Service or the Privacy Policy change, you see **Review the updated terms** the next time you sign in.

1. Read the summary of each change. The titles open the full documents.
2. Tick "I have read and accept the updated Terms of Service and Privacy Policy".
3. Click **Accept and continue**.

Until you accept, other pages send you back here. Support and the legal pages still open.

## Forgot your password

1. On the login page, click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email and click **Email me a code**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code and a **New password**, then click **Reset password**.

"Password reset" confirms it. Your other sessions are signed out.

## Verify your email

Buying, downloading, publishing, uploading builds and cashing out need a verified email. The exception is a free project whose developer turned on [anonymous downloads](../anonymous-downloads.md), which anyone can download without an account. Until you verify, a banner says "Verify your email to publish games, upload builds, download, and buy."

1. Click **Verify now** in the banner, or open Settings and choose **Verify email**.
2. Click **Send code**.
3. Enter the code and click **Verify**.

## Log out

Open the account menu and click **Log out**. On a developer's page, you are asked "Log out of Blazium Games on this browser?" first.
