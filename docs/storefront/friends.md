# Friends

![The Friends page](/img/storefront/friends.png)

Open **Friends** from the account menu.

## Add a friend

1. Type their username in **Username**. A leading `@` is fine.
2. Click **Send request**.

The page says "Request sent to @name". If they had already sent you a request, you become friends right away. Requests you sent are listed under **Sent requests**, where **Cancel** withdraws one.

## Answer a request

Incoming requests are under **Requests**. Click **Accept** or **Decline**.

## See what friends play

- **Playing now** lists friends in a game right now.
- **Your friends** shows each friend as **Playing** a game, **Online** or **Offline**.
- **Played in the last 14 days** lists recent games.

The home page shows the same recent games in **Friends are playing**.

**Remove** takes someone off your list straight away. There is no confirmation.

## Activity sharing

Under **Activity sharing** you choose whether friends see what you play:

- **Stop sharing** hides your current and recent games from friends.
- **Share with friends** shows them again.
