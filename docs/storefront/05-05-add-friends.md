What can go wrong

- If you are signed out, opening Friends sends you to log in. The page you see is Welcome back. There is no signed-out friends page. Log in is a different guide.
- Picture 2 is the account. Picture 4 and picture 5 are other samples of the page. Do not mix the names.
- An empty Send request shows Please fill out this field from the browser. The page does not show its own error under the box.
- These pictures do not show Share with friends. Sharing is on, so the button is Stop sharing.
- Remove, Cancel, Accept, and Decline were not used. The pictures do not show the list after those buttons.
