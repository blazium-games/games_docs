# Profile and settings

![Account settings with the Profile card](/img/storefront/settings.png)

Open **Settings** from the account menu. The side menu has **Account**, **Linked accounts**, **Authenticator**, **Verify email**, **Wallet**, **MCP** and **Copyright notices**.

## Profile

- **Upload avatar**: a square image from 256×256 to 512×512 pixels, up to 2 MB (PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP). Without one, your initials are shown.
- **Username**: your developer page lives at `username.blazium.games`, so changing it changes your store links.
- **Display name**: the name shown on your page and in the header.
- **Timezone**: used for dates and times.

Click **Save**. "Settings saved" confirms it.

Your public page at `username.blazium.games` shows your avatar, display name, username and published projects. Click **View profile** in the account menu to see it.

## Developer mode

Turning on developer mode lets you publish games, mods and tools. Players don't need it. See [Developer mode](../developer-mode.md).

## Adult content

Adult listings (sexual content or nudity) are hidden by default, and always hidden from people who aren't signed in.

To see them:

1. Under **Adult content**, tick "I am 18 or older, and adult content is legal where I live".
2. Click **Show adult content**.

Adult listings then appear in Browse, on developer pages and on store pages, marked **18+**. **Hide adult content** turns them off again. You can still leave them out of a single search with **Hide adult content** in Browse's filters.

How developers label adult content is covered in [Content rules](../content-rules.md).

## Copyright notices

**Copyright notices** lists the paid copyright (DMCA) notices that name your content, with their status and any messages from our staff. It's empty unless someone has filed one. See [Copyright (DMCA) notices](./dmca.md#if-a-notice-names-your-content).

## Cookies

The first time you visit, a banner asks about analytics. **Accept** turns on Google Analytics and browser fingerprinting, which help us understand how game pages are used. **Decline** keeps them off. Sign-in cookies are always on because the site needs them.

To change your choice later, click **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page. The details are in [Permissions](../legal/permissions.md).
