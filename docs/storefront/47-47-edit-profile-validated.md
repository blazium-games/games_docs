What can go wrong

- Save was not used. Do not say the profile was saved.
- Upload avatar was not used. Do not say a file was chosen.
- The avatar line has no period. It reads Avatar (256x256 to 512x512, square, up to 2 MB)
- 256x256 and 512x512 have no spaces around the x.
- The Display name value is covered by the cookie bar. Do not fill it in.
- The lines covered by the cookie bar on picture 1 are not quoted. Do not fill them in.
- Decline was not used. Accept was not used. Cookie details was not used. Do not say a cookie choice was saved.
- The bar text was already on the page. It is not the result of a click.
- Picture 1 is the sign-in form. The profile fields are not in that frame. The address bar is not in these frames. These pictures do not show a URL.
- The header Log in is not the form button Log in. Sign up is not Create an account. The header GitHub, X, and Discord are not the form buttons.
- Pictures 2 and 3 have no header Log in and no header Sign up. They have no header GitHub, no header X, and no header Discord.
- The Username box reads pixel on picture 2 and nova on picture 3. Nothing was typed.
- The timezone reads America/New York on picture 2 and Europe/Berlin on picture 3. Do not convert either. America/New York has a space between New and York. Europe/Berlin has no space around the slash.
