What can go wrong

- Linked accounts was not used. Do not say a page opened.
- The lines covered by the cookie bar are not quoted. Do not fill them in. The heading covered by the cookie bar is not quoted. Do not fill it in.
- The scope names keep the printed spaces: users . read, tweet . read, and offline . access. Do not close those spaces. GET /2/users/me keeps the space after GET and no spaces around the slashes.
- Decline was not used. Accept was not used. Cookie details was not used. Do not say a cookie choice was saved.
- The bar text was already on the page. It is not the result of a click.
- The header Log in is not the heading X API Disclosure. Sign up is not the heading. The header GitHub, X, and Discord are not the heading. The header X is not Linked accounts.
