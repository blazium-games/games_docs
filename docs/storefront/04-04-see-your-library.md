What can go wrong

- If you are signed out, opening the library sends you to log in. The page you see is Welcome back. There is no signed-out library. Log in is a different guide.
- Download on a card opens that game's store page. It is not the desktop app. Download the desktop app, in the sentence under the heading, is guide 2.
- These pictures are not an empty library. The lines Your library is empty and Or download the Blazium Games app are not on them. The empty line on picture 6 is only No games match this filter.
- Moss Garden has no Play button. Play is only on Starfall Drift in this sample.
- Play opens the store page at an address ending in #play. That is not a Play section on the library page, and these pictures do not show a Play section on the store page.
