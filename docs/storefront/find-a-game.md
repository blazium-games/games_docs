# Find a game

## The home page

![The home page with the Featured game and the Friends are playing row](/img/storefront/home.png)

The [home page](https://blazium.games) is a set of shelves. Each shelf only lists games that have a clean download for the platform your browser reports, so a Linux visitor sees games that run on Linux. Shelves with nothing to show are hidden.

| Shelf | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Featured** | One standout game, shown large. |
| **New releases** | Games published in the last 30 days, newest first. |
| **Recently updated** | Games and apps with the newest builds. |
| **Made with Blazium** | Projects built with the Blazium engine. |
| **In development** | Games still in development or at the prototype stage. |
| **Play in your browser** | Games with a web build. |
| **From the community** | Games players have reviewed and tagged recently. |
| **Tools, mods & assets** | Tools, mods, plugins and assets for developers. |
| **Something for tonight** | Short games that launch reliably. |
| **Unheard of** | New games that few players have found so far. |

Shelves with a **See all** link open Browse with the matching filter already set.

When you are signed in and a friend has played something in the last 14 days, a **Friends are playing** row appears under Featured. See [Friends](./friends.md).

If a shelf fails to load, the page says "Some sections could not be loaded. Reload the page to try again."

## Game cards

Each card shows the title, the developer, a short tagline and the price (or **Free**). Depending on the game, it can also show:

- **Play in browser** (the game has a web build) or **Download**.
- **In development** or **Prototype**.
- How long a session usually lasts and how many players it supports.
- How it was made: **Made by people**, **People with AI agents** or **Mostly AI agents**, as the developer states it.
- **18+** for adult listings. These only appear after you [turn on adult content](./profile-and-settings.md#adult-content).

On Browse, a game with launch problems also shows **Launch problems reported**. Click the title or picture to open the store page, or the developer's name to open their page.

## Browse

![Browse with the filter panel open](/img/storefront/browse.png)

Open **Browse** in the header.

1. Type in **Search for games, mods and tools**. Results update as you type.
2. Pick a quick filter: **All**, **New**, **Updated**, **Multiplayer**, **Browser** or **Made with Blazium**. Picking one replaces the previous quick filter.
3. Pick one or more types: **Game**, **Application**, **Mod**, **Plugin**, **Tool**, **Game asset** or **Development asset**. Click a type again to turn it off.
4. Change the order with the sort menu: **Best match**, **Newest** or **Recently updated**.

Click **More filters** for more options. Each group opens when you click it, and its summary says how many options are shown or hidden.

- **Genre**, **Tone** and **AI use** let you pick what to show and also what to hide.
- **Session length**, **Network**, **Platform** and **Made with** narrow the results to what you pick.
- **Content warnings** only hides: tick **Hide violence**, **Hide flashing lights** and so on.
- The text boxes filter by tags, hide tags, and a number of players.
- **Hide adult content** (signed in only) leaves adult listings out of this search even if you have turned them on.

A dot on **More filters** means one of these is set. **Clear all** resets everything.

If nothing matches, the page says "Nothing matches these filters. Try fewer filters or clear them all."

## Mods and tools for a game

A game's store page can have **Tools and utilities** and **Mods and plugins** rows. Items marked **From the developer** come from the game's own developer; **Community** items come from other people.

**See all** opens a page with every mod or tool for that game, split into **From the developer** and **From the community**. A mod's store page shows whether it works with the game's current build, and **How to install** explains where its files go.

## The developer's page

Each developer has a page at `username.blazium.games` with their **Games**, **In development** and **Tools, mods & assets**. Adult listings are hidden there too unless you have turned them on.
