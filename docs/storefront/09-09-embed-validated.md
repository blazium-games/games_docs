What can go wrong

- There is no copy button. The HTML is the text in the box. These pictures do not show a copied state.
- The iframe line is one line. It only looks like two lines because the box wraps it.
- The iframe addresses are samples on localhost.
- View & Buy, Download, and More on Blazium Games were not used.
- The X was not used.
- Signed out, you stay on the page. The dialog does not add an email field.
- Report, Press kit, the review form, and the beta line are other guides.
- The account chip is not in these pictures.
