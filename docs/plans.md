# Free, Premium, and Business

Each game or application has its own plan. A new game is Free. The public store page does not show the plan. Only the owner and an accepted admin can see it, on the project's Plan page. Either of them can start, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel it.

The card is charged to the person who subscribes, monthly or annually, through Stripe. The marketplace fee is not added on top. Cash-out is 8% + $0.25 on Free, 7% + $0.25 on Premium, and 6% + $0.25 on Business. The wallet uses the best plan the account owns. The per-sale fee stays $0.25 + 8%.

Blazium is the marketplace. The buyer pays Blazium. Blazium keeps $0.25 + 8% of the price. The rest is the creator's earnings. A Premium or Business subscription is charged to the subscriber and is not transferred to an Express account. A project cannot charge money until its owner has finished Stripe payout setup. A price of 0 stays allowed.

| Plan | Price | History an owner or admin can read |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 45 days |
| Premium | $29/month or $278.40/year | 360 days |
| Business | $99/month or $950.40/year | 5 years |

A year is 20% off twelve times the monthly price, so Premium is 27840 cents and Business is 95040 cents. Business includes everything Premium includes, and Premium includes everything Free includes. Premium adds lobby settings, achievement and stats settings, and more than one OAuth application. Business adds the anticheat settings for that game. Free and Premium do not include anticheat settings. This page does not sell anticheat and does not set a license price.

The window covers analytics events, including funnel series and owner stats, crash report list and detail, and bug ticket list and detail. Funnel, achievement, and stat definitions stay. The bug intake caps stay. A Free game can still receive new crashes and bug reports. Developer tools that call these private routes see the same cutoff.

Rows are kept until they are 5 years old, so a later upgrade can read them again. A daily job then deletes analytics events, crash reports, and bug tickets older than 5 years.

A year is 20% off twelve times the monthly price. An upgrade, including monthly to annual, charges the new price immediately and does not credit unused time. A downgrade, annual to monthly, or a cancel to Free takes effect at the end of the period already paid. Until then the higher plan stays in force.

A failed renewal is retried every 48 hours. The third failure emails the payer and the owner. Fifteen days after the original due time, the game becomes Free and the subscription is canceled. A successful retry before that clears the failure and keeps the paid plan.
