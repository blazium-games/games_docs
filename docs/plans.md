# Free, Grow, and Scale

Each game or application has its own plan. A new game is Free. The public store page does not show the plan. Only the owner and an accepted admin can see it, on the project's Plan page. Either of them can start, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel it.

The card is charged to the person who subscribes, monthly or annually, through Stripe. The marketplace fee is not added on top. On every plan, Blazium keeps $0.25 + 8% of a sale. A cash-out costs another 8% + $0.25 on Free, 7% + $0.25 on Grow, or 6% + $0.25 on Scale, from the best plan the account owns. Buyers pay card processing.

Blazium is the marketplace. The buyer pays Blazium. Blazium keeps $0.25 + 8% of the price. The rest is the creator's earnings. A Grow or Scale subscription is charged to the subscriber and is not transferred to an Express account. A project cannot charge money until its owner has finished Stripe payout setup. A price of 0 stays allowed.

| Plan | Price | History an owner or admin can read |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 45 days |
| Grow | $29 per game per month | 360 days |
| Scale | $99 per game per month | 5 years |

Grow includes everything Free includes. Scale includes everything Grow includes. The window covers analytics events, including funnel series and owner stats, crash report list and detail, and bug ticket list and detail. Funnel, achievement, and stat definitions stay. The bug intake caps stay. A Free game can still receive new crashes and bug reports. Developer tools that call these private routes see the same cutoff.

Rows are kept until they are 5 years old, so a later upgrade can read them again. A daily job then deletes analytics events, crash reports, and bug tickets older than 5 years.

## What each plan adds

Free includes a store page (draft, private, link-only, or public), selling (editions, bundles, donations, keys, and patron tiers), builds on stable, beta, and dev with virus scanning and rollback, the browser player and Discord Activities, crash reports tied to each build, the in-game bug reporter when you turn it on, analytics and funnels, and MCP for Cursor, VS Code, and Claude Code, plus the CLI. Distribution on Free is the Blazium store, the web player, and BlaziumLauncher. Lobby limits on Free are still being finalized.

Grow adds lobbies (relay, or scripted with Luau), STUN/TURN relay, achievement and stats settings, more than one sign-in app per game, and 360 days of analytics, crash, and bug history. Grow also includes distribution beyond the Blazium store, web player, and launcher: Discord Activities are already on every plan, and Grow adds YouTube Playables, TikTok, WhatsApp, CrazyGames, Facebook, Google Play / Android, Windows Store, Epic Games Store, Steam, Game Jolt, and iOS. For Steam, that store still gets the info and permissions it requires. Epic and the other stores work the same way.

Scale adds anticheat settings for that game. Anticheat is by request. Free and Grow do not include anticheat settings. This page does not sell anticheat and does not set a license price. Scale history is 5 years.

One sign-in application is included on Free. Another one needs Grow ($29/mo per game); Scale includes it.

## Changes and failed renewals

An upgrade, including monthly to annual, charges the new price immediately and does not credit unused time. A downgrade, annual to monthly, or a cancel to Free takes effect at the end of the period already paid. Until then the higher plan stays in force.

A failed renewal is retried every 48 hours. The third failure emails the payer and the owner. Fifteen days after the original due time, the game becomes Free and the subscription is canceled. A successful retry before that clears the failure and keeps the paid plan.
